# Saved to Serve — Creative TikTok Variations

All five videos are approximately 28 seconds and use the same voice-over.

## Recording script

The more the work of God grows, the more hearts and hands it requires.

We are saved to serve. That’s what we believe.

At Renewed Life International, there is a place for everyone to serve.

Do you have the gift? The heart? The willingness?

Then take your next step.

Scan the QR code or follow the link in the caption, and register to join one of these departments—or another ministry where God can use you.

## Delivery

Warm, purposeful and inviting. Emphasize **everyone** and deliver “The gift? The heart? The willingness?” as three distinct questions.
